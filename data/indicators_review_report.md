## Summary

- **23,988 observations** for 21 indicators and the 54 states, 2000–2025; **12,607 creditor rows** of public external debt.
- **Editions read:** Human Development Report 2025 (via Our World in Data); IMF WEO October 2026; SIPRI, via World Bank WDI, updated 2026-10-08; World Bank International Debt Statistics, updated 2025-12-03; World Bank WDI, updated 2026-10-08.
- **IMF estimates:** 64 figures for 2025 fill gaps in the World Bank series (IMF WEO October 2026); projections (2026 on) are never stored.
- **Human Development Index: read from Our World in Data's copy.** UNDP's own download (`hdr.undp.org`) did not answer (connections were reset), so the pipeline used Our World in Data's republication of the same UNDP series (HDR 2025, 1990–2023).
