## Sources used in this build

- **Price:** World Bank Pink Sheet monthly average (IMF PCPS as cross-check). Series definition (World Bank): Gold, spot average of daily rates, from June 2025; previously (UK), 99.5% fine, London afternoon fixing, average of daily rates
- **Production and in-ground reserves:** USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 (gold chapter) and the USGS Minerals Yearbook country chapters (.xlsx editions since 2016). The British Geological Survey and the World Gold Council are not used (owner's decision, 2026-10-18).
- **Central bank holdings:** IMF International Liquidity (one call for 54 countries).
- **Trade:** UN Comtrade, HS 7108 without 7108.20 (0 API calls in the last run).
